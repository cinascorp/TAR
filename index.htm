T∆R

The Airlines Reconstruction

Overview

T∆R is a real-time aerial traffic recognition and tracking system. It ingests live ADS-B / MLAT / FLARM feeds,cross-checks every contact against the military fleet index,classifies it (Passenger,Commercial,Private,Helicopter,Military Fighter / Transport / Bomber,Drone/UAV,Glider or High-Altitude),and renders it on an interactive map with altitude-based color encoding.

Altitude Color Spectrum

Each color step represents 1500 metersof altitude (0 – 12,0 m standard band):

  • ■#666666Gray — ground level
  • ■#FFA500Orange — 0 – 1500 m
  • ■#FFFF00Yellow — 1500 – 3000 m
  • ■#00FF00Green — 4500 – 6000 m
  • ■#0000FFBlue — 6000 – 7500 m
  • ■#4B0082Indigo — 7500 – 9000 m
  • ■#800080Purple — 9000 – 10500 m
  • ■#FF00FFFuchsia — 10500 – 12000 m

High-Altitude Contacts (>30 km):Pinkand Cyanare reserved for stratospheric / near-space vehicles:balloons,classified reconnaissance platforms,experimental UAVs. Color alternates per-contact via altitude hash.

Category Filters

  • Passenger— Scheduled airline flights (IATA-style callsigns)
  • Commercial— Cargo / charter / uncategorized civilian
  • Private— General aviation,GA tail-number patterns
  • Helicopter— Rotorcraft (low speed+low altitude+HELI callsign)
  • Military— Verified via MIL-SYNC hex DB+callsign prefixes+mil serials
  • Fighter— F-15/16/22/35,Typhoon,Rafale,Su-27/35,MiG-29… (pulsing reticle)
  • Mil Transport— C-5/C-17/C-130,A400M,KC-135/10/46,IL-76,AN-124…
  • Bomber— B-1/B-2/B-52/B-21 (flying wing)
  • Drone / UAV— Unmanned aerial vehicles incl. military UAV types
  • Glider— Sailplanes (GLID type code,D-K / PH-numbered registrations)
  • High-Altitude— Contacts above 30 km (Pink/Cyan)

Buttons:Show All· Hide All· Civilian· Militaryonly · UAVonly.

Symbols

  • Fighter jet — targeting reticle,pulses red
  • Heavy military transport / tanker (T-tail,4 engines)
  • Bomber — flying wing,slow red pulse
  • Glider / sailplane — cyan,long slender wings
  • Helicopter (red tint=military rotorcraft)
  • Drone / UAV (quad-X shape)
  • Airplane icon (color-coded by altitude) — Passenger / Commercial / Private;red tint=other military
  • Pink / Cyan airplane — High-Altitude contact (>30 km)

Controls

  • ☰— Open Settings &Filters panel
  • L— Toggle dark / light map tiles
  • ?— Open this Help panel
  • FA / EN— Switch language (English ⇄ فارسی)
  • MIL-SYNC— Force re-sync of the military DB
  • Clicka flight marker — open details popup &toggle track
  • Refresh— Force data refresh
  • Auto Update— Toggle background polling
  • Proxy— Route requests through CORS proxies
  • Search— Filter flights by callsign / hex / registration
