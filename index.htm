T∆R

TARGETED AERIAL RECOGNITION

Overview

T∆R is a real-time aerial traffic recognition and tracking system. It ingests live ADS-B / MLAT / FLARM feeds, classifies each contact into a category (Passenger, Commercial, Private, Helicopter, Military, Drone/UAV, or High-Altitude), and renders it on an interactive map with altitude-based color encoding.

Altitude Color Spectrum

Each color step in the standard spectrum represents 700 meters of altitude. The standard spectrum covers 0 – 4,900 m (7 steps):

0m700m1.4k2.1k2.8k3.5k4.2k4.9k>49km>49km
  • ■ #666666 Gray — Ground level / 0 m
  • ■ #FFA500 Orange — 0 – 700 m
  • ■ #FFFF00 Yellow — 700 – 1,400 m
  • ■ #00FF00 Green — 1,400 – 2,100 m
  • ■ #0000FF Blue — 2,100 – 2,800 m
  • ■ #4B0082 Indigo — 2,800 – 3,500 m
  • ■ #800080 Purple — 3,500 – 4,200 m
  • ■ #FF00FF Fuchsia — 4,200 – 4,900 m

High-Altitude Contacts (> 49 km):

Two special colors — Pink (#FF69B4) and Cyan (#00FFFF) — are reserved for contacts flying above 49 kilometers (49,000 m) from Earth's surface. These represent stratospheric / near-space vehicles: high-altitude balloons, classified reconnaissance platforms, experimental UAVs, and exo-atmospheric test flights. They appear at the end of the spectrum bar and are toggleable via the High-Alt >49km category chip.

Color alternates between pink and cyan based on altitude hash to visually distinguish individual contacts in the same extreme-altitude band.

Category Filters

Use the category chips in the right panel to selectively display each class of traffic. Each chip toggles independently — click to enable/disable. Quick presets:

  • Passenger — Scheduled airline flights (IATA-style callsigns)
  • Commercial — Cargo / charter / uncategorized civilian
  • Private — General aviation, GA tail-number patterns
  • Helicopter — Rotorcraft (low speed + low altitude + HELI callsign)
  • Military — Armed forces callsigns and prefixes (NATO, USAF, RAF, etc.)
  • Drone / UAV — Unmanned aerial vehicles (low speed, very low altitude)
  • High-Altitude — Contacts above 49 km (Pink/Cyan)

Buttons: Show All · Hide All · Civilian (pax + commercial + private + heli) · Military only · UAV only.

Performance Notes

Markers are pre-filtered by category before feature creation. Animations run on a single requestAnimationFrame loop. Tracks are capped at 500 points per contact. Color blending is cached per-hex to avoid recomputation. Only the active group slice (26,000 contacts) is rendered at a time.
